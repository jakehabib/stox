What else makes a player a person

Four proposals rendered against the real dark palette. Everything on this page is drawn from a seed with inline SVG — no images, no fonts, no network. Reload and every face, crest and number comes back identical, exactly as the app's avatars already do. Nothing here removes anything that is on the screen today.

PROPOSAL 1

Portraits that carry a body and an age

Small–Medium · no schema

The avatar generator already knows the player's age but uses it only to grey his hair. It never sees weightLb, heightIn or position — so a 330 lb nose tackle and a 178 lb kicker get the same neck, the same shoulders and the same jaw, standing next to a line of text that says otherwise. Crusader Kings III's portraits work because they agree with the data. Below, the same seeds, the same faces, with mass and age moved into the silhouette.

Same seed, today vs. proposed
What changed
  • Mass — shoulder span, neck width, jaw width and cheek fullness scale off weightLb against the position's own band. Reads at 20px, before a single feature resolves.
  • Age — hairline recession from ~30, forehead and nasolabial lines, heavier lower lid, on top of the greying that already exists.
  • Uniform — a shoulder stripe in the club's accent, and the jersey number on the chest at 72px and up.
  • Every existing hair style, beard, eye, brow, nose, mouth and face shape is untouched. This is strictly more parameters, never fewer.

PROPOSAL 2

Jersey numbers

Small–Medium · one nullable column

A number is the cheapest identity primitive in sport, and it is how people actually refer to players. Assigned by position convention (QB 1–19, OL 50–79, WR 80–89…), unique on a roster, seeded from the player id. PlayerHero.tsx already accepts a jersey prop and renders it — nothing passes one. The strategic reason to do it: retired numbers are impossible without it, and a franchise museum without retired numbers is a table.

PROPOSAL 3

“Your guy” — provenance, tenure and a hometown on his own page

Small · no schema

The database already knows you drafted him in the sixth round, that he has been here four years, and what he has won. His page says none of it. DraftPick.playerId is a unique column; the GM Career page lists your picks but the picks themselves don't know they're yours. This is the endowment effect with the evidence filed in the wrong drawer — and it is a one-query fix.

PROPOSAL 5

Crests that are marks, not monograms

Medium · no schema · mostly drawing

Today all 32 crests are the same object — a shape, a pattern, and the abbreviation in a black box. The three letters do 100% of the differentiating. Add a seeded emblem layer and make the crest size-responsive: under 28px it renders exactly as it does today, because at 20px a mark is mud and the letters are the only thing that reads. Above 40px the mark leads.

PROPOSAL 4

The Franchise Museum

Medium · no schema

lib/gen/leagueHistory.ts already synthesises 16–24 years of internally-consistent past for all 32 clubs, with franchises that have eras rather than fixed personalities — dynasties decay, doormats get their decade. It is audited for consistency before it is handed back. Almost none of it is visible below the league level. The generation is done; only the room is missing.
